- Use `assets/header.svg` for the deep-blue, white, and pink research identity. Keep biography and research text readable if external badges or statistics cards fail to load.
- Statistics and visitor-count widgets may depend on third-party services and should remain supplemental to the profile content.
- Contribution animations and commit feeds have been removed at the owner's request, together with their update script and scheduled workflow. Do not add them back automatically.
- Shields.io and github-readme-stats supply badges and cards and may be cached or temporarily unavailable; the local banner and plain homepage/contact links remain usable independently.
