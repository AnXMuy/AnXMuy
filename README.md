<h2>Hi, I’m Zixuan <sub>(Andrew)</sub></h2>

I’m an undergraduate student in Artificial Intelligence at <a href="https://www.xjtu.edu.cn/">Xi’an Jiaotong University</a>, in the Young Gifted Program (2021 cohort) and Artificial Intelligence Experimental Class (2023 cohort). I will begin a Computer Science PhD at <a href="https://www.sii.edu.cn/">Shanghai Innovation Institute</a> and <a href="https://cs.sjtu.edu.cn/">Shanghai Jiao Tong University</a> in September 2027, advised by <a href="https://chenxie95.github.io/">Xie Chen</a>. I currently work with the X-LANCE Lab.

My research focuses on **Audio-Visual Multimodal Intelligence**: how systems understand and represent the world through audio and video, and how they interact and collaborate with people.

<sub>音视频多模态智能 · 理解世界，并构建能够与人自然交互的系统。</sub>

## Research directions

| | Focus |
|:--|:--|
| **Multimodal understanding & representation** | Combining audio and video to understand events and their context, and learning representations that retain information across modalities. |
| **Interactive speech systems** | Building systems that can interpret ongoing speech, respond to corrections, and preserve a speaker’s final intent. |
| **Human–AI interaction & collaboration** | Enabling audio-visual systems to interpret human intent, learn from feedback, and support natural interaction. |

Earlier in my research, I worked on **remote-sensing image interpretation**, including localized image captioning and open-vocabulary perception.
